Detailing With a Purpose

Shine for a Cause is a fully mobile detailing company built in Fort Collins. We show up to your driveway or office with everything we need: water, power, and professional-grade products, then leave your vehicle looking better than the day you bought it.

But the shine is only half the story. A portion of every single booking is donated to local recovery organizations in Northern Colorado: AA and NA groups, the SAFE Foundation, and direct sponsorships of individuals working through rehab programs. When your car shines, so does the community.

Automotive Ceramic Coatings

Experience the best long‑term coating

Trusted by Boeing and auto manufacturers

Shine for a Cause uses a top professional-grade, crystal-clear liquid nano ceramic coating, the same class of protection found on Boeing aircraft. Once cured, our 9H ceramic coatings transform on the surface into a long-lasting, durable yet flexible glass shield. Think of it as an additional clear coat, with 6x the hardness and self-healing properties.
