Detailing With a Purpose

Shine for a Cause is a fully mobile detailing company built in Fort Collins. We show up to your driveway or office with everything we need — water, power, and professional-grade products — and leave your vehicle looking better than the day you bought it.

But the shine is only half the story. A portion of every single booking is donated to local recovery organizations in Northern Colorado — AA and NA groups, the SAFE Foundation, and direct sponsorships of individuals working through rehab programs. When your car shines, so does the community.
