Ceramic Coating

UV rays fade your paint, road salt eats into it, bird droppings etch through the clear coat, and years of daily driving leave behind a dull, oxidized surface that no amount of washing can fix. A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied directly to your vehicle's paint that chemically bonds and hardens into a glass-like protective shell, shielding your investment for years and keeping that just-detailed look between appointments.

We're a System X Certified Installer, which means every coating comes with a manufacturer-backed warranty up to lifetime and is automatically reported to Carfax. That's documented proof of protection on your vehicle history, adding real, verifiable value when it's time to sell or trade in.
