WHAT IS CERAMIC COATING?

Think about the last time it rained right after you washed your car. Water spread across the paint, picking up every bit of dust and grime along the way. That's exactly what a ceramic coating puts a stop to.

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds directly to your vehicle's paint. Once it cures, it forms a layer that's harder than your factory clear coat — so water beads up and rolls right off, taking dirt and contaminants with it instead of letting them sit and eat away at your paint.

The result is a car that stays cleaner longer, looks deeper and glossier in the sun, and holds up against UV rays, bird droppings, road chemicals, and daily wear. Not for a few weeks like wax — for years. It's the most permanent paint protection available, and once it's on, maintenance is almost effortless.
